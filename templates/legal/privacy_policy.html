Privacy Policy

SWEAT CRM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When a person submits a Facebook or Instagram lead form, SWEAT CRM may receive information the person voluntarily provides. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Lead form responses and survey answers
  • Page, form, and campaign source information
  • Submission date and time

2. How We Use Information

Information received through lead forms is utilized to:

  • Respond to enquiries and appointment requests
  • Contact prospective customers regarding fitness programs, memberships, and trial sessions
  • Manage and organize incoming prospective client leads within SWEAT CRM
  • Assign enquiries to authorized staff members and representatives
  • Provide requested information, services, and membership details
  • Maintain operational CRM records

3. Meta / Facebook Lead Ads

SWEAT CRM may receive lead information from Meta Lead Ads after a user voluntarily submits an Instant Form on Facebook or Instagram and authorizes the relevant Page and application integration. This integration is designed exclusively to facilitate direct lead communication between prospective clients and the organization. Meta does not endorse, sponsor, or administer SWEAT or its CRM platform.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is only shared with authorized organization staff and vetted technical service providers strictly required to operate and maintain the CRM platform, subject to appropriate confidentiality and administrative controls.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, operational, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. While we maintain industry-standard security measures, no data transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be infallible.

6. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfill legitimate business, operational, customer support, and legal compliance obligations.

7. User Requests

Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information at any time by contacting the organization. We will review and process verifiable requests in accordance with applicable requirements.
