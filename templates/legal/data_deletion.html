Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 2026 — SWEAT CRM

This page explains how you can request the deletion of data that SWEAT CRM has received via its Facebook / Meta integration, or any other personal data held about you on our platform. We are committed to respecting your data rights under applicable law.

1. What Data We Hold

Through our Meta Lead Ads integration, SWEAT CRM may hold the following information about you:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number submitted via Facebook or Instagram Lead Ad forms
  • Your Facebook/Meta user ID (where applicable, used only to facilitate the integration)
  • Page access tokens and OAuth connection data stored on behalf of the connected business account
  • Lead form responses and campaign attribution metadata
  • CRM records created from lead form submissions (enquiries, follow-up tasks)

2. How to Revoke the Facebook Connection

If you authorized a SWEAT CRM tenant to receive your lead data via Facebook, you can revoke this authorization directly through Facebook at any time:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings
  2. Select Security and Login → Apps and Websites
  3. Find the SWEAT app or the relevant business Page integration
  4. Click Remove to revoke access

Revoking Facebook access prevents future data transmission. It does not automatically delete data already received by the connected business.

3. How to Request Deletion of Your Data

To request deletion of personal information held by SWEAT CRM, please submit a written request including:

  • Your full name as submitted in the lead form
  • Your email address and/or phone number associated with the enquiry
  • The name of the fitness centre or organization you enquired with (if known)
  • The approximate date of submission (if known)
  • A clear statement that you are requesting deletion of your personal data
Send your request to: {% if contact_email %}{{ contact_email }}{% else %}the SWEAT CRM platform support channel{% endif %}
Subject line: Data Deletion Request — Meta Lead Ads

We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and process verifiable deletion requests within 30 days, subject to the limitations below.

4. What We Will Delete

Upon receiving a verified deletion request, we will:

  • Remove your personal contact information (name, email, phone) from our CRM records
  • Delete or anonymize any lead import records associated with your submission
  • Remove any stored OAuth tokens linked to your Facebook account (if you were the connected business user)
  • Confirm completion of deletion in writing

5. Legitimate Retention Exceptions

Note: Certain records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business compliance purposes, including but not limited to financial transaction records, audit logs required under applicable regulations, and records subject to an active legal obligation. We will inform you if any part of your request cannot be fulfilled and the reason why.

6. Response Timeframes

  • Acknowledgement: Within 5 business days of receiving your request
  • Completion: Within 30 calendar days of identity verification
  • Extension: We may extend this period by up to 30 additional days for complex requests and will notify you accordingly
